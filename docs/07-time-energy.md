# Time Energy

Time Energy adds deterministic month and day layers for future dashboard and reporting features.

## Month Energy

Month Energy combines target-year context with calendar month context. It produces one reduced 1–22 energy for each month.

## Day Energy

Day Energy combines a date with its month energy and the day of month. It is designed for daily reflection surfaces, not deterministic prediction.

## Product use

These values can power:

- home dashboard highlights;
- monthly overview cards;
- daily reflection prompts;
- AI guide context;
- PDF report sections.
